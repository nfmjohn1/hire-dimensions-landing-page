# Hire Dimensions — HVAC Job Ad Audit Rubric

Brand: Hire Dimensions (hiredimensions.com). Audience: HVAC company owners/GMs.
Purpose: AI grades their real job posting; produce a scored report used on a live "hiring audit" call that soft-opens into HD recruiting (culture-fit A-players who stay).

## Philosophy (grade against this)
- Lead with what the candidate GETS; end with what you need.
- Specifics convert ("$28–$42/hr + van") — "competitive pay" fails.
- Hook must speak to employed-tech frustration or belonging, not HR template.
- No corporate jargon ("dynamic self-starter", "fast-paced environment").
- Title: searchable trade + level (e.g. "HVAC Service Technician — Residential"), not "Rock Star".
- Structure order: Title → Hook → Company → Offer (high) → Role → Who we're looking for (attitude then credentials) → Clear CTA.
- Attitude/culture before long cert lists. Lean requirements.

## Scoring dimensions (0–10 each; overall 0–100 weighted)
1. **Title clarity** (10%) — searchable trade + role type; not cute/vague
2. **Opening hook** (20%) — human, frustration/belonging, not corporate
3. **Offer specificity** (20%) — pay range, OT, van/tools, benefits visible early
4. **Structure / scannability** (10%) — benefits high; short bullets; right section order
5. **Role realism** (10%) — day-to-day picture, not legalese
6. **Candidate filter quality** (15%) — attitude first, lean must-haves, invites near-fits wisely
7. **CTA / friction** (10%) — one clear apply path; optional "what happens next"
8. **A-player appeal** (5%) — would an employed good tech stop scrolling?

Letter grades: 90–100 A, 80–89 B, 70–79 C, 60–69 D, <60 F.

## Report sections (always)
1. Overall score + letter + one-line verdict
2. Dimension scores with 1–2 sentence notes each
3. Top 5 fixes (ranked, specific to THEIR ad — quote snippets)
4. "Rewrite preview" — improved hook (2–4 sentences) + improved offer bullets (sample) — clearly labeled as draft for discussion on the audit call
5. Soft CTA: Book a free 20-min Hiring Ad Audit — we'll walk this report live and map how Hire Dimensions fills seats with people who stick

## Lead capture (required before full report OR gate full rewrite behind booking — product choice)
Preferred MVP flow:
1. Paste job ad (+ optional company name, city, role type, contact email/phone)
2. Instant overall score + top 3 issues on-screen (hook them)
3. Collect name, email, company, phone to unlock full PDF/report view
4. CTA button: "Book your live Hiring Ad Audit" (configurable Calendly/Cal.com URL env var)
5. Notify HD (webhook or email stub) with lead + scores + raw ad for sales follow-up

## Tone of report copy
Direct, trades-aware, no fluff. Sound like a sharp recruiting partner who understands HVAC shops — not a generic AI HR tool.
